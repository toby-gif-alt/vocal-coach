Prototype guide

From MusicXML to useful feedback.

  1. 1

    Export MusicXML. In your notation software, export the full score as MusicXML or compressed MXL.

  2. 2

    Choose exactly what you sing. Vocal Coach never assumes a part named “Voice” exists.

  3. 3

    Practise, then assess. Practice can play the guide. Assessment mutes it and listens through your microphone.

  4. 4

    Review the shape. See how each note started, settled, and sustained.

This first prototype assesses fundamental pitch only. It does not yet grade rhythm, vibrato, breath, diction, or voice type.
