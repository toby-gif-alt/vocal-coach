Prototype guide

From MusicXML to useful feedback.

  1. 1

    Export MusicXML. In your notation software, export the full score as MusicXML or compressed MXL.

  2. 2

    Choose exactly what you sing. Vocal Coach never assumes a part named “Voice” exists.

  3. 3

    Choose how much help you need. Practice plays the guide, Assisted Assessment scores with the guide, and Assessment scores with accompaniment alone.

  4. 4

    Review the shape. Your accepted pitch stays on the written staff, while Your Vocal Coach connects strengths and priorities to specific notes.

This prototype assesses fundamental pitch only. It does not yet grade rhythm, vibrato, breath, diction, or voice type.
