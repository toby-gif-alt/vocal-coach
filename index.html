Install Vocal Coach

Open Vocal Coach in Safari. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, then Add. If your browser offers Open as Web App, leave it enabled. Finish the offline download before practising without internet.

Change singing part

Choose the line you want to sing.

Change singing part?

This will stop and finish your current take before changing parts. Its recording and trace will be cleared when you choose a new part.

Find your starting note

Listen to your starting note, then sing it at a comfortable pitch. Choose the octave that suits your voice.

Listening…

Let's check your microphone

  • Find a reasonably quiet place.
  • Position your device at a comfortable distance.
  • Use headphones where possible.
  • You can sing any comfortable note.

Check your microphone

A quick room-and-voice check is recommended each time you reopen Vocal Coach, especially if your room, microphone or distance has changed.

Both modes can play your music and vocal guide without a microphone.
